About FoodBridge

Who We Are

FoookdLoop is an AI-powered waste management platform developed to simplify waste collection, improve recycling efficiency, and encourage sustainable practices.

Our platform connects households, waste collectors, recycling centers, and administrators through one intelligent system.

Our Mission

To create cleaner cities by making waste segregation, collection, and recycling smarter, faster, and more transparent using Artificial Intelligence.

Our Vision

A future where every piece of waste is properly managed, helping communities reduce pollution and build a sustainable environment.

Key Features

  • AI-powered waste classification
  • Smart collection scheduling
  • Role-based dashboards
  • Reward system for recycling
  • Real-time notifications
  • Analytics and reporting
